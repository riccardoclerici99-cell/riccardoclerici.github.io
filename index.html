Riccardo Clerici's horror style is deeply rooted in the masters of global horror cinema. Capable of ranging from gothic atmospheres to raw, industrial soundscapes, Clerici's notable scores include Il Primo e Ultimo Bagliore di Vita, Devara, and Sige, among others. He has also collaborated with NABA (Nuova Accademia di Belle Arti) and the Campiello Giovani Festival—for which he provided the soundtrack for Alberto Varsalona's winning story—and in 2026, he scored the music for the Officina Film Festival

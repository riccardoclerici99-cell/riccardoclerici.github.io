ABOUT ME

Adaptive music
for every media

I deeply believe in the power of visual storytelling. My approach is to immerse myself into the heart of the plot to shape the perfect sonic world, ensuring that every soundtrack I create is unique and indissolubly tied to the project it accompanies.

My musical journey began as a self-taught musician, exploring the piano and bass. Driven by the desire to refine my artistic vision, I moved to Milan to attend the CPM Music Institute, where I earned my degree in Composition.
Parallel to my academic studies, I began establishing myself in the artistic scene. In 2022, I won the Premio Campiello Giovani, a recognition that reflects my passion for narrative, and in 2025 I scored the soundtrack for the short film Devara, directed by Akash Ram Illa.

Having always been fascinated by cinema — with a particular affinity for genre films ranging from horror to thriller — I found in film scoring the perfect synthesis of my passions. Today, I continue to dedicate myself to researching and evolving my craft, constantly striving to deepen my understanding of the psychological aspects behind composition and the emotional impact of sound on moving images.
